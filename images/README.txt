=============================================================================
HOW TO ADD YOUR OWN BACKGROUND PHOTOS:
=============================================================================

You can add any photo or image you want by simply placing them in this folder!

1. hero-bg.jpg        -> Displays during Chapter 1 (Opening System Vision)
2. chapter-bg-1.jpg   -> Displays during Chapter 2 & 3 (Engineering & Core Foundations)
3. chapter-bg-2.jpg   -> Displays during Chapter 4 & Final Hero Reveal
4. profile.jpg        -> (Optional) Your profile / avatar photo

Supported formats: .jpg, .png, .webp, .jpeg

You can also edit index.html or css/style.css to change image paths to any image file!
=============================================================================
